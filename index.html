FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers to what carriers ask us most.

What does a truck dispatcher do?

A dispatcher finds loads, negotiates rates with brokers, handles paperwork, and keeps communication running so you can focus on driving.

Do I need my own MC authority?

Yes, you generally need active operating authority (MC/DOT), insurance, and your carrier documents in order. If you’re unsure where you stand, call us and we’ll walk you through it.

How much does dispatching cost?

We charge a percentage of the loads we book for you. The exact rate depends on your equipment and fleet size, so contact us for a clear quote.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

We’ll explain our terms clearly before you start. Ask us about the agreement when you contact us so you know exactly what to expect.

Do you book loads without asking me?

No. You approve every load before we confirm it.

What equipment do you dispatch?

Dry van, reefer, flatbed, power only, box truck, and hotshot.

What areas do you cover?

We dispatch across all 48 states. Tell us your preferred lanes and home base and we’ll plan around them.

What documents will I need?

Typically your MC/DOT authority, W-9, certificate of insurance (COI), and a copy of your authority letter. We’ll guide you through it during onboarding.

Who handles detention and layover?

We help you document and request detention or layover pay from the broker. Keep your timestamps and we’ll follow up on your behalf.
